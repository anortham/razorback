## Corrected autonomy boundary to start after plan approval

### WHAT
Adjusted the prior autonomy patch after user feedback: `writing-plans` now retains explicit plan approval as the final human gate before autonomous execution. `brainstorming` keeps pre-plan design/spec approval gates. Post-approval execution still avoids extra reviewer-choice prompts by defaulting reviewer choice to `none` unless approval/spec requests codex, gemini, or claude.

### WHY
The intended behavior is not 'autonomy from the first request.' It is 'autonomy after the plan has been approved.' The earlier patch overcorrected by treating the original implementation request as enough consent to execute the plan.

### HOW
Updated regression tests in `tests/autonomous-process-gates.test.mjs` to require pre-plan approval gates and plan approval, while still forbidding post-approval prompts like `External review before PR?`. Restored the plan approval handoff text and kept blocker-taxonomy routing for in-run uncertainty.

### IMPACT
Razorback now preserves human control over design/spec/plan approval, then runs without unnecessary stops once the approved plan starts.
