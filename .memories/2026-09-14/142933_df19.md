## Task 1: Worker Subagent Self-Spawn Prohibition

- **WHAT:** Forbid implementer subagents and code reviewers from spawning child subagents or reviewers.
- **WHY:** Subagents spawning subagents or reviewers creates duplicate reviews, runaway token costs, and confused state.
- **HOW:** Added `## You Do Not Dispatch Subagents` section to `implementer-prompt.md`, `fix-prompt.md`, and `code-reviewer.md`. Added rationalization row to `skills/subagent-driven-development/SKILL.md`. Added guard test `tests/subagent-no-spawn.test.mjs`.
- **IMPACT:** Guarantees worker subagents perform assigned work directly without spawning child subagents.
