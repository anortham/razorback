## Task 3: Interactive Worktree Removal Refusal Guard

- **WHAT:** Guard against data loss during interactive worktree cleanup when uncommitted or untracked files exist.
- **WHY:** Blindly passing `--force` when `git worktree remove` refuses destroys uncommitted work permanently.
- **HOW:** Updated `skills/finishing-a-development-branch/references/interactive-mode.md` Step 5 to handle removal refusal by inspecting untracked files with `status --porcelain -uall` and prompting the user with three explicit options (commit, move, delete). - **IMPACT:** Prevents accidental deletion of uncommitted scratch/notes/code in worktrees.
