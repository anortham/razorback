## WHAT

Approved a lightweight design addendum at `docs/plans/2026-08-31-grok-review-completion-design.md` for the cluster of external-review failures.

## WHY

A real Grok 4.6 review returned schema-valid planning placeholders without inspecting repository content. A separate Claude review passed a 276 KB diff as one positional argument, exposing the longstanding per-argument transport limit. Grok's read-only sandbox also fails before session creation on this host because `/run/podman` is root-only.

## HOW

The shared review schema will require completed-review evidence and reject `needs-attention` with no findings. A small executable validator will normalize envelopes and enforce the contract. Grok standalone review receives a predeclared two-call budget only for one initial turn plus one `-c` continuation of that same session when validation rejects an incomplete result. Large review payloads move through temporary files or stdin. Claude keeps `Read,Grep,Glob`; reviewers do not gain Bash. Sandbox startup remains terminal for its campaign, with the already-approved explicit new-campaign `--sandbox off` fallback.

## IMPACT

Review success will mean evidence exists, not merely that the CLI emitted valid JSON. Large diffs no longer depend on shell positional-argument limits, and the read-only reviewer boundary remains intact.
