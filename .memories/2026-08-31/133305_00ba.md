## Cut 0.36.0 — Grok reviews complete

### WHAT

Bumped all five manifests to 0.36.0 on top of `26150b1`, the two-phase Grok review fix.

### WHY

0.35.0 hardened transport and left the actual failure in place. `--json-schema` collapses Grok's agent loop to one turn, so every Grok review returned a placeholder no matter how small the prompt was. This release is the first one where a Grok review can finish.

### HOW

Free-form review pass, then a resume of the same session for the schema. Plus two validator fixes (last-object parsing for multi-turn `.text`, and rejection of bundle-only citations) and the `--sandbox workspace` recovery.

### IMPACT

Grok joins Claude as a reviewer that returns usable findings. The `.razorback-review/` bundle can no longer be cited as evidence that a review happened.
