- **WHY:** TODO "Default workflow reduction": clear work proceeds directly; ambiguous requests get a question; consequential choices keep explicit attention.

Kept the three paths and the architectural spec/approval flow. Replaced 5 tests that enforced the old ceremony with behavior checks, plus a new bootstrap test.
