## Made code-kb and Goldfish optional (workflow reduction item 3)

- **WHAT:** The instruction-tier ruleset (canonical plus 5 host copies), the `using-razorback` toolchain, and `subagent-toolchain.md` now call code-kb "an optional retrieval aid". A new rule says a missing or stale index never blocks work. The `check-rule-copies.mjs` invariants changed to match. Worker, fix, and reviewer prompts and the execution skills ask for evidence from current source instead of code-kb calls. `harvesting-debt` now searches the literal marker text with `rg`, because markers are comments that code-kb does not index. Checkpoints follow Goldfish's selective policy: a consequential decision, a surprising failure, or unfinished work that needs a handoff. A warranted checkpoint is still written before its commit and staged with it. Recovery works without Goldfish, and the finishing report commit needs no separate checkpoint. Site, README, harness docs, and the Claude and Codex manifests updated.
- **WHY:** TODO item 3; aligns with code-kb's plan (`2026-09-23-retrieval-value-and-optional-routing.md`) and Goldfish's selective-memory backlog.
- **HOW:** Kept the capability table, the API-shape rule, and the external-reviewer exception (the lead's code-kb-backed bundle stays for pre-merge review). Left the frozen Cursor manifest and `CLAUDE.md` (item 4) unchanged.
