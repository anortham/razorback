## Codified Gate-Aware Delegation Policy

- **WHAT:** Added repo-root `RAZORBACK.md` and updated planning, subagent, Codex routing, dispatch, pre-merge, and README policy docs.
- **WHY:** Feedback from a tiered delegation run showed that docs-plus-evidence tasks with acceptance semantics need stronger routing than mechanical medium.
- **HOW:** Mechanical workers are excluded from failing tests, replay evidence, metrics, and acceptance gates. Plans now request gate invariants, hard-gate/report-only metric labels, stop-on-failed-assigned-verification behavior, and a `gate-review` route. Worker templates no longer let workers own affected-change, branch-gate, or specialist gates.
- **IMPACT:** Future Razorback-generated plans and prompts should preserve the distinction between rote edits and evidence interpretation across harnesses.
