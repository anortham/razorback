## Fixed parallel-lead-commit plan progress durability

### WHAT

Updated `skills/subagent-driven-development/SKILL.md` so `parallel-lead-commit` lead bookkeeping now ticks the task's plan checkbox before staging, stages the reviewed task's owned files plus the plan file, commits, and only then writes durable progress with the real commit SHA.

### WHY

The previous wording told the lead to commit the reviewed files first, then tick plan checkboxes. Because lead staging was intentionally scoped, the plan progress edit could remain uncommitted or dirty after the task commit, undermining resume/release evidence.

### HOW

- Added a regression test in `tests/codex-parallelism-contract.test.mjs` that failed on the old ordering.
- Patched the lead staging and review-pass instructions in `subagent-driven-development` to make the order explicit.
- Kept the durable-progress rule that the progress line is written only after the real commit SHA exists.

### IMPACT

Parallel lead commits now preserve both implementation edits and tracked plan progress in the same scoped lead commit, while keeping `.razorback/sdd/progress.md` as post-commit scratch recovery state.
