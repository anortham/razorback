## BRE-44 through BRE-50 design doubt pass

**WHAT:** Completed the required one-call cross-model doubt pass with Claude for the proposed Razorback hardening release. User approved a basename plus 12-hex path digest for SDD workspaces and approved archiving the stale agent-tier brief.

**WHY:** Reviewer isolation changes a security boundary. The design needed an adversarial check before implementation.

**HOW:** Lead gathered Miller and Goldfish evidence, then sent one read-only refutation prompt under the current no-policy-declared rule. **IMPACT:** The design must add real CLI or OS read confinement, or narrow its guarantee explicitly. No production files changed. Campaign closed after the single allowed invocation.
