## WHAT

Hardened Razorback's goal-independent autonomy contract across brainstorming, blocker classification, security recovery, reviewer policy checks, branch finishing, and SDD pre-merge wording. Added focused workflow-facing regression guards and folded the approved task into the design document.

## WHY

Today's security and review changes introduced premature stop paths: routine reversible brainstorming details could manufacture questions, no-policy reviewer dispatches could be blocked by an absent allowlist, and first-run scanner or branch-gate failures could be classified as blockers before safe recovery was attempted. Long-running autonomous work must continue with or without `/goal`, `/loop`, or another goal runner while preserving security hard gates.

## HOW

- Kept `blocker-taxonomy.md` as the canonical stop/continue policy and made recovery-before-classification explicit.
- Kept `security-review/SKILL.md` as the canonical security policy, requiring diagnose/repair/rerun without weakening secret or high/critical CVE gates.
- Made reviewer allowlist checks conditional on an existing external-model policy block; the no-policy path proceeds with the loud morning-report note.
- Bounded brainstorming questions to material product, safety, scope, or architecture decisions and recorded reversible defaults.
- Corrected SDD to describe one general pass plus one security pass, each once, with no post-fix external re-review.
- Preserved the existing architecture: no new seams or abstractions.

## IMPACT

Autonomous sessions now press through recoverable failures and reversible decisions without depending on a goal runner or waking the user prematurely. Provider denial, destructive-action boundaries, pre-plan approval gates, and security severity gates remain intact.
