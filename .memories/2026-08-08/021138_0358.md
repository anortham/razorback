## Approved goal-independent autonomy contract

### WHAT

Created `docs/plans/2026-08-07-autonomy-contract-hardening-design.md` covering the four audited defects: brainstorming question scope, no-policy reviewer dispatch, security failure recovery, and stale pre-merge pass wording.

### WHY

The user confirmed long-running autonomy is Razorback's top workflow priority and explicitly approved the design. Goal runners may improve persistence but must never be a prerequisite for agents to continue.

### HOW

The blocker taxonomy stays canonical. Brainstorming infers reversible details; security policy checks are conditional on a declared block; branch/security failures diagnose, repair, and rerun before blocker classification; callers accurately describe the dual-pass review.

### IMPACT

The worktree is ready for TDD implementation. Baseline `npm test` passes 220/220. The next step is to add regression guards and prove they fail against the current contracts.
