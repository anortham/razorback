## Wrote the bounded review campaigns design

### WHAT

Created the canonical design and visual digest:

- `docs/plans/2026-08-10-bounded-review-campaigns-design.md`
- `docs/plans/2026-08-10-bounded-review-campaigns-design.html`

### WHY

A prior autonomous run executed 26 Claude+Grok review pairs because per-invocation uncapping was not paired with a universal campaign-level contract.

### HOW

- Ran RED pressure scenarios for one-, two-, and three-model availability.
- Preserved ordinary lead-only review while keeping explicit external/cross-model requirements blocking when unavailable.
- Separated reviewer depth from campaign repetition with immutable round and invocation counters.
- Limited discovery to Round 1, fix-diff confirmation to Round 2, and Round 3 to a lead-verified critical/high fix regression.
- Rejected voting; the lead closes every finding on recorded file/symbol/test evidence.
- Ran one bounded Claude doubt pass and adopted its strongest objections: count actual invocations, label evidence strength honestly, keep pre-merge stricter, remove disputes as a Round 3 trigger.

### IMPACT

The written spec is ready for user review. Production skills remain unchanged until approval. The implementation will add `managing-review-campaigns` and integrate it into reviewer CLIs and orchestration with guard and pressure tests.
