## Razorback 0.31.0 release state verified

### WHAT
Fast-forwarded `main` through the bounded review campaign implementation and bumped all five version-bearing manifests from 0.30.0 to 0.31.0.

### WHY
This minor release adds a new canonical campaign contract and integrates it into reviewer CLIs, convergence, pre-merge review, execution recovery, and reporting.

### HOW
The release keeps individual reviewer invocations deep while bounding the campaign with immutable participants, external-call budgets, a three-round maximum, evidence-based severity, and terminal `campaign_closed: yes` states. One-model ordinary review remains supported; selected external reviewers run once with at most one shared targeted confirmer.
