## Diagnosed runaway external-review campaign

### WHAT

Investigated whether Razorback 0.29.0's removal of reviewer turn/spend caps caused an autonomous run to execute 26 reviewer pairs.

### WHY

Razorback needs deep individual reviews without allowing outer orchestration to reopen discovery indefinitely.

### HOW

- `b901518` removed `--max-turns` and spend caps from each CLI invocation; it did not authorize repeated invocations.
- `cross-model-convergence` has had a default four-round discovery cap since `b202da3` (2026-06-12).
- `pre-merge-review` explicitly runs general and security once each and forbids review/fix/re-review loops.
- The gap is policy locality and ambiguous wording: CLI skills say reviews have “no caps,” while campaign-level limits live only in specific orchestrators. Ad-hoc multi-reviewer orchestration can therefore miss or misread the outer bound.

### IMPACT

The recommended design is a universal review-campaign closure contract: keep each invocation internally uncapped, freeze discovery after one campaign, allow one scoped confirmation campaign, reserve at most one final targeted arbitration for a newly introduced critical/high defect, and make the lead the evidence-based closure authority. A third model may arbitrate one named severe dispute but cannot reopen discovery.
