## Task 3 workflow integration complete

### WHAT
Integrated the bounded campaign contract into cross-model convergence, pre-merge review, both execution paths, recovery checkpoints, and the morning report. Updated both pre-merge reviewer prompt recipes and added `tests/review-campaign-integration.test.mjs`.

### WHY
The campaign boundary must survive real callers, compaction, and provider-specific prompts. Otherwise a local retry rule or goal continuation can reopen review after the cap.

### HOW
Cross-model discovery runs each deliberately selected different-model reviewer once and declares an immutable budget of selected external reviewers plus one optional confirmer. Pre-merge retains exactly general plus security once each, captures stderr on the original call, blocks on malformed output without retry, and confirms fixes locally. Goal recovery treats every `campaign_closed: yes` state as terminal.

### IMPACT
One-, two-, and three-model availability is supported without enrolling every installed model. The original Claude+Grok pattern is bounded to one discovery call each plus at most one shared targeted confirmation, not repeated pairs.
